# Apex Technologies Inc. — Q3 Financial Performance & Operational Summary
Fiscal Quarter Ended: September 30, 2024
Report Published: October 18, 2024
Investor Relations Contact: ir@apextechnologies.example.com

---

### Executive Overview
Apex Technologies delivered a record-setting third quarter for fiscal year 2024, driven by accelerated adoption of our Enterprise AI Platform and expansion within Fortune 500 accounts. Total consolidated revenue reached $482.5 million, representing a 28.4% year-over-year increase compared to $375.8 million in Q3 2023.

### Key Financial Highlights
1. Revenue Breakdown:
   - Subscription Software (SaaS): $395.2 million (81.9% of total revenue), growing 32.1% YoY.
   - Professional Services & Implementation: $58.1 million (12.0% of total revenue), growing 11.4% YoY.
   - Hardware Appliances & Edge Devices: $29.2 million (6.1% of total revenue), growing 4.8% YoY.
2. Profitability and Margins:
   - GAAP Gross Profit was $347.4 million, yielding a GAAP Gross Margin of 72.0%, an increase of 140 basis points from 70.6% in the prior year period.
   - Non-GAAP Operating Income reached $114.8 million, up 39.2% YoY, producing a Non-GAAP Operating Margin of 23.8%.
   - Net Income stood at $86.3 million, or $0.54 diluted earnings per share (EPS), beating analyst consensus expectations of $0.48 EPS.
3. Cash Flow and Balance Sheet:
   - Operating cash flow for Q3 2024 was $138.2 million, compared to $98.4 million in Q3 2023.
   - Free cash flow (FCF) rose to $121.7 million, delivering a robust 25.2% FCF margin.
   - Cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities totaled $1.42 billion as of September 30, 2024. The company maintains zero outstanding long-term debt.

### Customer Metrics & Retention
- Total active enterprise customers grew to 3,840, an increase of 460 net new customers over the past 12 months.
- Customers contributing greater than $100,000 in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR) reached 712, up 34% YoY.
- Net Revenue Retention (NRR) rate remained industry-leading at 122%, underscoring strong product stickiness and net seat expansion within the installed base.
- Gross Revenue Churn was maintained at an all-time low of 1.4% annualized.

### Research & Development (R&D) Investments
R&D expenditures in Q3 totaled $84.2 million, or 17.5% of total revenue. Strategic R&D initiatives concentrated on:
- Next-generation multimodal RAG search engine with low-latency vector quantization.
- Autonomous AI agent workflows for automated financial reconciliation and contract audit.
- SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 continuous compliance automated telemetry.

### Q4 2024 Financial Guidance
For the fourth quarter of fiscal 2024, management projects:
- Total revenue in the range of $510.0 million to $520.0 million, representing YoY growth of 24% to 26%.
- Non-GAAP operating income in the range of $122.0 million to $126.0 million.
- Diluted EPS between $0.57 and $0.60 based on approximately 161 million diluted shares outstanding.
- Full-year FY2024 revenue guidance raised to $1.865 billion - $1.875 billion.
